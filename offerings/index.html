01

Workflow advisory

Choose the work worth changing.

Turn a broad AI ambition into a practical sequence of business changes. Start with the workflows, decisions and constraints that matter to your organisation.

Leaders who need an investment decision and a credible starting point.

What we shape together

  • Workflow and opportunity map
  • Build, buy and integration assessment
  • Business baseline, success measures and prioritised roadmap
For example

Decide whether a recurring reconciliation problem needs a better process, an existing tool or a purpose-built agent.

02

Business workflow agents

Move work across the handoffs.

Build agents around a defined job: gather context, prepare work, use tools and stop for the decisions that require a person. Keep calculations and business rules in inspectable code.

Operations, finance, sales and service teams working across disconnected systems.

What we shape together

  • A bounded workflow and working pilot
  • Source-grounded outputs and exception paths
  • Evaluation set, approval gates and operating handover
For example

Prepare a customer brief, triage a service request or assemble evidence for an invoice exception.

03

MCPs, CLIs & connected systems

Give agents the right tools.

Expose useful business capabilities through APIs, Model Context Protocol servers and command-line tools. Scope each action to its user, its data and its purpose.

Teams with valuable systems that agents cannot safely use yet.

What we shape together

  • Focused tool contracts and integration adapters
  • Authentication, scoped permissions and audit events
  • Retry behaviour, observability and integration documentation
For example

Read permitted CRM records, prepare a ticket update and request approval before a write. MCP supplies the connection; the implementation supplies the controls.

04

AI-assisted software delivery

Make delivery repeatable.

Create a disciplined path from specification to working software, using coding agents alongside engineers. Make acceptance, review and release part of the system.

Engineering teams building internal tools or modernising repeatable delivery work.

What we shape together

  • Repository and development-environment foundations
  • Agent-ready specifications, tests and review workflows
  • CI checks, controlled releases and team enablement
For example

Take a well-defined internal application through implementation, browser checks, human review and a repeatable release.

05

Evaluation, governance & adoption

Build the confidence to keep using it.

Define what an agent may do, how its work will be judged and who takes over when it fails. Bring the people responsible for the workflow into the design.

Teams moving from a promising demo toward operational use.

What we shape together

  • Task evaluations and failure-case testing
  • Human approval, monitoring and escalation design
  • Cost visibility, ownership and adoption playbook
For example

Test whether a workflow uses the right sources, respects permissions, handles an unavailable tool and stays within its cost budget.
