Eywa · Prototype

Agents propose.
People approve.

Build company knowledge that lasts beyond a conversation. Eywa’s prototype keeps approved facts, their sources and their history, so people and agents can use them over time. In the demonstrated flow, every agent answers only from what’s approved and permitted for it.

Discuss Eywa ↗

Approved knowledge, kept over time.

Long-term company knowledge is part of the prototype’s design. Its demonstrated version history keeps the previous approved value, links it to its replacement and records the source, decision and effective date. Permitted people and agents can refer to that shared record across later questions. Knowledge changes through explicit proposals and review; the prototype does not automatically learn from watching people.

A proposal is not an answer.

An agent proposes

A new fact arrives with its source. A conflicting change waits for review.

A person approves

The reviewer checks the proposed change and records a decision with a reason.

Agents use the approved fact

In the demonstrated flow, permitted agents cite the approved value. The proposed value is not used before approval.

When a stale fact costs real money.

For operations, finance, compliance and revenue leaders in mid-sized and larger organisations. These behaviours are shown in the supplied prototype demonstrations.

One approval, cited by agents

After a person approves the change, the demo agents cite the same approved version and its source.

Unsafe inputs held back

The documented demonstrations show prompt-injection attempts and sensitive-number examples blocked from approved answers. This is test evidence, not a promise to catch every attack.

Customer boundaries checked

The prototype tests show requests for another customer’s information refused. A real deployment still needs its own access-control review.

Decisions on the record

The demonstrated audit history records the proposal, source and approval decision, preserving the earlier approved value.

Two people before write-back

A separate person approves a comment returning to the mock source. The demo does not execute payments or make automatic field changes.

A prototype to explore together.

Eywa is exploratory software demonstrated with synthetic data. It is not generally available. A real deployment needs a separate review of fit, permissions and readiness.
