Eywa · Built product · 11 domain packs

Company knowledge.
Kept current.

Eywa keeps company knowledge current for people and AI agents. Contribute a fact, check its source, review a change, and keep the approved version and its history. Eleven domain packs give that shared knowledge the vocabulary and structure of your work.

Approved knowledge, kept over time.

Eywa stores facts with their sources, versions and decisions. A new approved value replaces the current one while keeping its history, including when it took effect and when it was recorded. People and agents can ask about current knowledge or what was known at an earlier time. Updates are submitted explicitly through the web console, CLI, REST API or MCP.

Agents propose. People approve.

A proposal is not an answer.

An agent proposes

New facts from agents and conflicting changes wait for review with their sources.

A person approves

The reviewer checks the proposed change and records a decision with a reason.

Agents use the approved fact

Permitted agents cite the admitted version and its source. A proposal waiting for review is kept out of their answers.

Built to keep knowledge useful.

One record across interfaces

People use the console, CLI or API. Agents contribute and ask through MCP. They work with the same versioned knowledge.

Sources beside answers

Answers carry references to the facts and versions used. Source checks and review decisions make the knowledge inspectable.

Checks before admission

Source verification, conflict routing, and screens for injection attempts and sensitive-number patterns are implemented. Their effectiveness still needs evaluation against your data.

Current and historical answers

Keep earlier versions and ask what was known at a particular time. Changes retain their source and decision history.

Controlled write-back

Where supported, request a source comment or note and have a different authorised person approve it. Eywa does not execute payments or automatically change source fields.

Knowledge you can take with you

Workspace export includes facts, versions and source references in a structured format.

See the product. Check the fit.

Eywa is built software with domain-specific demonstrations. We can walk through the relevant pack and agree the data, integrations, permissions and deployment scope with your team.

People’s access depends on their role, team and topic. Agents currently read company-wide facts within assigned topics; team-restricted and curator-only facts stay unavailable to them.

Many source systems in the demonstrations are mocks or fixtures. Regulated packs carry no compliance attestation; their evaluations use synthetic data. Availability and rollout terms are discussed directly.
