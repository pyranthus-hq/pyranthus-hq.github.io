25:T4b4,What is a CompanyOS? It's the operating system of a company run by people and AI agents.
Companies used to run on documents and people, now AI agents do real work alongside them.
In Microsoft's 2025 Work Trend Index, 46% of leaders say their companies are using agents to fully automate workflows.
Think of it in layers. Agents and apps on top, workflows and connectors to every system in the middle,
and at the base, one trusted, permissioned, current body of company knowledge.
Cloudflare, Eragon and Wonderful are among those building layers like these.
The weak point is the base. Facts go stale or conflict. Memory gets poisoned.
OWASP lists that as a top risk for AI agents. Permissions must mirror the company and every
change needs someone accountable. Much of what a company knows lives in people's heads,
notes and inboxes, and in each AI agent's private memory. When someone leaves, or an agent is reset,
it's gone. When two copies disagree, nobody knows which is right. Eywa gives it one secure,
shared home. Each fact passes a validation gate, fingerprinted, checked, approved by a person.
