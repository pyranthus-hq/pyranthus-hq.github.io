Eywa/Domain packs

Eywa · 11 built domain packs

The language of your work.

A domain pack gives Eywa the topics, vocabulary and starting structure your team uses. It includes review and visibility defaults, starter questions and sample knowledge. The same knowledge and governance workflows run underneath.

Built packs. Specific jobs.

These packs exist in the product. Finance has its own recorded walkthrough; the other ten have dedicated domain demonstrations. The demonstrations use synthetic data, with staged comparisons and mock or fixture systems labelled.

Finance

Close calendars, payment terms, expected receipts, treasury policies and controls.

“Which expected-payment date is current, and who approved the change?”

Customer support

Support tiers, releases, escalation routes and known issues.

“Which escalation target and workaround should the team use now?”

Engineering & platform

Service ownership, platform configuration, incidents and release procedures.

“Who owns this service today, and who owned it before the handover?”

Sales & field teams

Accounts, opportunities, competitor facts and solution knowledge.

“Has the competitor’s pricing changed since this battlecard was written?”

Corporate advisory

Mandates, valuation assumptions, financing terms and deal calendars.

“Which valuation assumption did the deal team approve?”

Demonstrated with fictional mandates. Team visibility does not establish a regulatory information barrier.

Insurance

Underwriting guidelines, referral triggers, forms and operating procedures.

“What is the current referral threshold, and when did it change?”

Operations knowledge; policyholder and claim-file data are outside this pack’s scope.

Legal operations

Matter ownership, recorded contract terms, notice periods and firm policies.

“What notice period was approved, and which version did the team rely on?”

Records approved terms; does not interpret contracts or provide legal advice.

Medical practice operations

Practice procedures, referral contacts, scheduling rules and staff protocols.

“Which referral contact is current across the practice?”

Operations only; patient records and clinical advice are outside this pack’s scope.

Wealth management operations

Advisor responsibilities, fee schedules, review procedures and meeting-note facts.

“Which approved fee-schedule version applied at a particular time?”

Does not compute or send invoices, provide investment advice or replace regulated records systems.

Hospitality operations

Property operations, outlets, internal policies, events and maintenance.

“Has the reopening date changed, and do both teams have the current version?”

Internal knowledge; guest messages, rates and availability are outside this pack’s scope.

Property content

Fees, amenities, stay policies and facts used in website and listing content.

“Which approved fee should the website and listing drafts use?”

Content facts; does not publish directly to booking channels or manage reservations.

Shared controls. Domain-specific context.

Topics and vocabulary

Organise facts around the subjects and terms the team works with. Pack guidance helps extract and name those facts.

Review and visibility defaults

Start with pack-specific defaults for who can read a topic and when first values need review. Administrators configure the workspace.

A practical starting point

Use sample sources and starter questions to evaluate the workflow, then agree the data, integrations and operating rules for your organisation.

Pack guidance is context for the product, not a separate security guarantee. Regulated packs carry no compliance attestation and their demonstrations use synthetic data. They do not replace the existing systems of record.
